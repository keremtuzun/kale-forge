01

Robots from requirements

Describe the constraints, payload, envelope, speed, budget, manufacturing method, and Kale Forge synthesizes an original robot: chassis, drivetrain, intakes, shooters, turrets, elevators, arms, climbers, electronics, and a BOM with real part numbers.

Design a robot
02

Engineered, not guessed

Each design comes with the math worked out on real parts, MK4i/MK5i swerve ratios, Kraken X60 motor curves, PDH channel and breaker assignments, wire gauges, mass and center-of-gravity, so you can see whether it holds together, not just what it looks like.

03

Nothing arrives in Onshape flattened

Designs publish as generated parametric source, not a mesh: every part is a real feature, every measure is a named variable beside the call that uses it, and a gear's pitch diameter is written as its derivation from the tooth count — edit the count and the geometry moves. The frame parameters rebuild the whole layout.

Workflow

Designed around the work, not dashboard decoration.

Upload or describe the system, inspect the generated evidence, revise the design, and export files for the engineering tools you already use.

  1. 1

    Describe

    Pick the season, then state what you know — frame size, named hardware, the job the robot has to do. Explicit facts are specification, never suggestion.

  2. 2

    Synthesize

    Everything left open resolves against a real parts catalog and the season's rule budget, so no answer can exceed what would pass inspection.

  3. 3

    Inspect

    Every design ships its evidence: part-level CAD, a channel-by-channel power budget, a mass roll-up, a cut list, and a rule check that reports rather than reassures.

  4. 4

    Publish

    Send the design to Onshape as parametric source with every dimension still editable, or download the whole engineering package.

Trust and operation

A public product with an explicit boundary.

Anyone can create an account and use Kale Forge from a browser. Accounts, original files, analysis, generated artifacts, and design histories are stored by Kale Forge and kept private to their owner.

  • Passwords are strongly hashed; browser sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies.
  • Every account sees only its own projects and generated designs.
  • Uploaded originals are retained so generated results remain traceable.
  • No personal server or cloud-provider account is required.
  • Engineering warnings remain visible throughout the product.
