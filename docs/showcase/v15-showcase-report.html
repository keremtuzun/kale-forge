The honest read

Ten for ten through the model with schema-valid output. What that does and doesn't prove:

What it demonstrably does

  • Builds mechanisms it never saw in CAD training — serpentine indexer, tunnel intake, paddle-wheel hopper, winch carriage — at full feature depth.
  • Treats the season as a constraint system: the same 28×28 ask is legal in 2025 and resized in 2026, with the rule cited.
  • Solves the shot instead of remembering it: every shooter clears its required exit velocity from goal height and range.
  • Respects explicit hardware — MK5n, Kraken X60, tank drive, "no shooter" — as specification, not suggestion.

Where it substitutes

  • The telescoping-box elevator became a belt-rigged cascade — the nearest trained architecture, not the asked-for one.
  • "Four-bar linkage arm" landed as a four-bar intake plus a shoulder arm — right vocabulary, wrong subsystem.
  • Elevator travel converges to ~29 in — derived from the start-height cap, but the model rarely argues for more via extension.

What the rule check caught

  • R502 twice: six-motor west-coast drivebases exceed the four-propulsion-motor limit — reported with the fix.
  • R103 once, on the heaviest build.
  • Caught means reported: passing is "nothing caught here," never a claim of legality. The manual stays the authority.
